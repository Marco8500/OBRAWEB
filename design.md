## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the serene, poetic atmosphere of an artisanal floral atelier. It unites organic botanical beauty with high-end editorial commerce. The design direction merges **warm editorial minimalism** with **tactile softness**, crafting an intimate boutique experience that feels human, sensory, and meticulously arranged.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Character:** Poetical, artisanal, grounded, fragrant, and meticulously curated.
- **Target Audience:** Design-conscious consumers, celebratory gift buyers, wedding clients, and lifestyle enthusiasts seeking mindful luxury and sustainable floral craftsmanship.
- **Emotional Response:** Evokes calm, sensory delight, the tactile warmth of petals and raw linen, and uncompromising confidence in bespoke craftsmanship.

### Visual Aesthetic & Principles
- **Air & Composition:** Generous breathing room, asymmetric rhythm reminiscent of Ikebana arrangements, and unhurried whitespace.
- **Sensory Texture:** Clean cream canvases layered with velvety sage, powder rose accents, and warm terracotta punctuation.
- **Organic Geometry:** Rounded silhouettes evoking smooth river stones, ceramic vases, and delicate petals, avoiding harsh corners in favor of soft, natural arcs.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from living flora, terracotta potting vessels, and soft natural fiber paper.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#2D4B3E` - Deep Sage Evergreen):** The foundational anchor representing foliage and structure. Used for high-emphasis buttons, key interactive headers, and dominant editorial callouts.
- **Secondary (`#E8B4B8` - Warm Dusty Rose):** A tender, floral petal tone used for accent containers, badge backgrounds, atmospheric tints, and seasonal highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D97762` - Soft Terracotta):** Earthy accent tone for high-conversion prompts, badge alerts, sales flags, active filters, and subtle decorative accents.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1F2923` - Soft Charcoal Moss):** High-legibility dark neutral replacing pure black to preserve organic softness across all body typography and icons.
- **Canvas & Surface Tones:**
  - Base Background: Warm Alabaster Cream (`#FAF7F2`)
  - Elevated Container: Petal Cream (`#F4E3E5` at 40% tint or pure `#FFFFFF`)
  - Subtle Border / Hairline: Botanical Mist (`#E2DDD4`)

### Usage Ratios
- **60% Cream & White:** Canvas, open spaces, and backdrop panels.
- **25% Deep Sage & Charcoal:** Typography, iconography, solid action buttons, and footer sections.
- **10% Dusty Rose:** Soft surface cards, secondary pill buttons, and decorative badges.
- **5% Terracotta:** Urgent tags, checkout actions, and seasonal accents.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary elegance of Playfair Display with the functional clarity of Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the editorial refinement of luxury print publications and floral monographs. Titles should maintain relaxed letter spacing (`0px` to `-0.01em`) and organic casing (sentence case preferred over all-caps). Italic variants are reserved for floral variety Latin names, collection descriptions, or romantic accents.
- **Body, UI & Meta (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Its rounded geometry bridges modern readability with organic warmth. Ensures high scanning speed for botanical care instructions, cart checkouts, and shipping specifications.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-emphasis badges and product tags can leverage light letter-spacing (`+0.04em`) when rendered in small uppercase configurations to retain a curated boutique packaging feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows an adaptive editorial grid tailored for visual presentation and seamless e-commerce transactions.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas bounded at `1380px` to maintain generous outer space.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Full bleed photo sections transition to edge-to-edge carousels with negative margins.

### Spacing Principles
- **Asymmetrical Compositions:** Editorial hero sections pair oversized left-aligned serif headlines with offset floral imagery framed by `space-xl` gaps.
- **E-Commerce Density:** Product catalogs use comfortable gaps (`space-lg` desktop, `space-md` mobile) ensuring individual arrangements stand distinct without visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through tactile ambient shadows and warm surface layers, avoiding artificial stark drops.

### Elevation Principles
- **Natural Ambient Shadows:** Diffused, multi-layered shadows tinted with warm organic undertones rather than cold grays:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(45, 75, 62, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.03)`
  - *Hover Elevation:* `0 14px 34px -4px rgba(45, 75, 62, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.04)`
  - *Floating Cart / Sticky Bar:* `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.12)`
- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is primarily established by placing crisp `#FFFFFF` product cards atop the warm `#FAF7F2` canvas, bounded by subtle `1px` borders in `#E2DDD4`.
- **Glass & Frosting:** Header bars and bottom action trays utilize backdrop blur (`16px`) with 85% opacity `#FAF7F2` to preserve continuity with the underlying floral photography.

## Shapes

The shape system draws inspiration from natural ceramic vessels, organic foliage curves, and river stones. 

### Corner Radius System
- **Base (0.5rem / 8px):** Minor controls, badge tags, and input internal elements.
- **Large (1rem / 16px):** Primary form inputs, dropdown containers, small cards, and standard action buttons.
- **Extra Large (1.5rem - 2rem / 24px - 32px):** Feature cards, product showcase frames, modals, and promotional banners.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Status badges, delivery time indicators, category selector pills, and icon floating buttons.
- **Organic Cutouts:** Optional asymmetrical treatment on featured editorial images where top-left and bottom-right corners feature `2rem` radii while diagonal corners retain `0.75rem`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid Deep Sage (`#2D4B3E`) background, crisp warm white typography (`#FAF7F2`), roundedness `rounded-xl` (1rem), subtle hover transition with a gentle scale (`1.01`) and deepening shadow.
- **Secondary Button:** Warm Dusty Rose (`#E8B4B8` at 30% background), `#2D4B3E` label, borderless or wrapped in `#E8B4B8` hairline.
- **Tertiary / Outlined Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#2D4B3E`, charcoal moss text, transitioning to light sage wash on press.
- **Icon Actions:** Circular pill (`rounded-full`) with `#FFFFFF` translucent ground, used for wishlist heart icons and gallery zoom triggers.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Card Structure:** 
  - Background: Pure `#FFFFFF` on `#FAF7F2` canvas.
  - Image Ratio: 4:5 vertical aspect ratio with gentle `rounded-2xl` corners and `overflow-hidden`.
  - Floating Badges: Top-left pill tags for "Seasonal Harvest", "Bespoke", or "Dried".
  - Content: Editorial serif product name (`headline-sm`), botanical description in muted gray, price in bold sans-serif, and subtle "+" instant cart button.
- **Bundle / Subscription Cards:** Wrapped with soft terracotta or dusty rose hairline border to denote special artisan status.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Generous height (48px - 52px), background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2DDD4`, `rounded-xl`. 
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to Deep Sage (`#2D4B3E`) with a soft `3px` spread of `rgba(45, 75, 62, 0.15)`.
- **Date & Delivery Time Picker:** Calendar widget highlighted with pill shapes, terracotta accents for delivery days, and sage background for the chosen slot.

### Chips & Category Filters
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `space-xs space-md`. Inactive state sits on cream surface with subtle `#E2DDD4` outline. Active state transitions to solid `#2D4B3E` with white text, or soft rose `#F4E3E5` with sage text for secondary tags.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** `20px` square with `rounded-md` (6px) corners. Inactive state: `1.5px` border in `#1F2923` at 40% opacity. Checked state: Solid `#2D4B3E` with an organic off-white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Smooth circular tokens with an inset terracotta or sage dot on an off-white background.

### Atelier-Specific Components
- **Floral Freshness Indicator:** Pill badge containing a soft green leaf icon and text ("Hand-cut today" / "3-5 day vase life guarantee").
- **Stem Count & Palette Previewer:** Swatch row depicting the live flower variety colors (e.g., ranunculus, eucalyptus, garden rose) using soft circular color chips.
- **Gift Note Drawer:** Soft paper-textured textarea with cursive editorial prompt styling and a wax-seal inspired confirmation badge.
